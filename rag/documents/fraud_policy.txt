FRAUD DETECTION SECURITY POLICY
================================

1. TRANSACTION RISK THRESHOLDS
--------------------------------
- LOW RISK (< 40% probability): Transaction processed normally.
- MEDIUM RISK (40-70% probability): Transaction flagged for manual review. MFA may be requested.
- HIGH RISK (> 70% probability): Account temporarily locked. User must verify identity to unlock.

2. AUTOMATED LOCK TRIGGERS
---------------------------
An account is automatically locked when any of the following conditions are met:
- Transaction amount exceeds 5x the user's historical average.
- High-value transaction (> $1,000) from a new, unrecognized device.
- Geographic location shift combined with a new recipient.
- Transaction velocity exceeds 5 transactions in a short period.
- Transaction occurring between 10 PM and 5 AM local time.
- New account (< 30 days old) making a high-value transfer (> $500).

3. IDENTITY VERIFICATION PROCESS
----------------------------------
When an account is locked:
- The user receives a notification explaining the reason.
- The user must verify their identity through the portal.
- Accepted verification: government ID upload, biometric confirmation, or OTP to registered phone.
- Upon successful verification, the lock is resolved with status RESOLVED.

4. FRAUD REPORTING
-------------------
If the user confirms the transaction was NOT made by them:
- They should click "Report Fraud" in the portal.
- The lock status is updated to FRAUD_CONFIRMED.
- The security team is alerted and initiates an investigation.
- The account remains blocked pending investigation.

5. COMMON FRAUD PATTERNS
-------------------------
- Card-not-present fraud: Transactions made without physical card, often in unusual locations.
- Account takeover: New device login followed by immediate high-value transfer.
- Velocity fraud: Multiple small transactions in rapid succession to test card validity.
- Geographic anomaly: Transaction in a location inconsistent with the user's known travel patterns.
- New recipient fraud: Sending money to a recipient never transacted with before, combined with high amounts.

6. USER RIGHTS
--------------
- Users have the right to understand why their transaction was flagged.
- Explanations are generated by our AI system and are available immediately.
- Users may appeal a lock decision by contacting support@sentinelfraud.com.
- All data is encrypted at rest using AES-256 encryption.
